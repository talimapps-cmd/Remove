Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Your privacy matters. This page explains what SnapClean does and does not collect.

Your photos

Photos you select are processed on your own device, inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers and we cannot see them.

AI model files

The first time you use the tool, your browser downloads the AI model and related code from third-party content delivery services. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as with any file download. Your photos are not sent to them.

Google Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type. This data is aggregated and does not include your photos. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Local storage

The site may store a small value in your browser (local storage) to estimate processing speed for the progress bar. It stays on your device and contains no personal information.

Accounts and personal information

SnapClean has no accounts and does not ask for personal information. If you message us, we use your message and contact details only to reply.

Children

SnapClean is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
